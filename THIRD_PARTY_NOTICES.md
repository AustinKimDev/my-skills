# Third-party support skills

The following support is redistributed under the MIT license. Each vendored source and each standalone bundle that contains it retains its `LICENSE`. Machine-readable source URLs, pinned license evidence and SHA-256 hashes of imported files are in [vendor/sources.json](vendor/sources.json). Standalone packages carry the relevant records in `embedded/SOURCES.json`.

| Upstream | Included support |
|---|---|
| [Jakub Krehel's skills](https://github.com/jakubkrehel/skills) | better-accessibility, better-layout, better-typography, better-colors, better-writing, better-ui, better-interface |
| [Emil Kowalski's skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills) | apple-design, emil-design-eng, review-animations, improve-animations |
| [im-not-ai](https://github.com/epoko77-ai/im-not-ai) | humanize-korean entrypoint and the references needed for UI-copy review and its cited rewriting guidance |

These are snapshots of previously installed files, including existing local adaptations. In particular, Korean UI-copy integration is a local addition, and some skill descriptions or scope instructions were already adapted to the local environment. Import hashes identify those exact snapshots; the license revision does not imply that every file matches that upstream commit.

Generated copies rename the discovery entrypoint to `GUIDE.md`, remove discovery frontmatter, add a scoped support notice and dependency map, and relocate Markdown links. Other supplied content and licenses are retained. Original copyright holders remain the authors of their material; these support skills are not claimed as original personal skills.

The browser, image-generation and component-library guides under `resources/` are separately maintained integration guidance. They are not copies of a provider's complete skill, installed executable, component package or authenticated session. Original font, image, icon and library notices already present in owned skills remain with those assets.
