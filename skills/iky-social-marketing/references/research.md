# Research notes for organic social marketing

Reviewed 2026-11-02. These are sources of ideas, not platform rules or guarantees. Recheck current platform guidance when decisions depend on it.

- [instagram-skills](https://github.com/sergebulaev/instagram-skills): plan a purposeful mix, make the opening clear, match a post to an audience and a primary action, and consider how formats support the value. Its installation, external publisher and per-post approval flow are not prerequisites and do not override the user's chosen workflow.
- [instagram-agent-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill): compare performance against a comparable account baseline; inspect the specificity of an opening. Its author reports that the hook scorer did not reliably distinguish hits from misses in a small YouTube Shorts sample (AUC 0.56), not an Instagram evaluation. Treat scores and fixed cutoffs as editing prompts, never predictions or causal evidence. Do not import its example results as the user's results.
- [Meta's Threads educational insights](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/find-your-community-with-new-threads-educational-insights/): Meta reports observations about replies, conversations and context with media. These are platform-level associations, not guarantees for an individual account; actual audience fit and user-imposed frequency limits take priority.
- [Meta's Threads Insights update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/03/new-threads-features-more-personalized-experience-you-control/): where available, Insights can show views, interactions, discovery and link visits. Availability varies by account and rollout; inspect the actual interface rather than assume access.
- [X guide for businesses](https://business.x.com/content/dam/business-twitter/en/resources/downloadables/guide-for-businesses-and-professionals.pdf): use a sustainable cadence suited to the account and experiment with useful organic posts. This is vendor guidance, not evidence that a cadence causes growth.
