# Caption audit

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Read the complete draft, audience, platform, available evidence and voice. Audit the opening, accurate product status, useful substance, readability, required conditions and one clear action. Flag unsupported claims or missing source facts separately from style suggestions. Preserve the author's meaning, dates, credits and restrictions; do not invent missing numbers.

Return only actionable findings with brief reasons, then a revised draft if requested or necessary. A platform display preview must be observed in its actual interface; if unavailable, call any text-length estimate provisional. Verify current limits only when they matter. Existing iky-x-korean-writing, iky-threads-korean-writing, iky-instagram-korean-writing and humanize-korean may be used for appropriate copy work. Avoid claims that a score detects AI or forecasts performance.
