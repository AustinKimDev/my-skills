# Apple website

Source: [Apple Korea homepage](https://www.apple.com/kr/), inspected 2026-09-06 through Aside Browser MCP at 1440 × 900 CSS px, device scale 2. This is a public marketing website, separate from Music and Wallet application patterns.

## Campaign opening

- Evidence: [initial viewport](captures/apple-web-home-20260906.png).
- Observed: compact global navigation above a full-width event visual. A luminous brand mark dominates the hero; a short centered headline, event timing, and one calendar action complete the proposition. The next product section begins below it.
- Borrow for: a launch or campaign with one message and starting action. Use a meaningful product/brand asset rather than a decorative object added to imitate Apple.
- Tradeoff: the tall visual delays service discovery; it fits announcements better than repeated operational tasks. Event artwork and dates are temporary content.

## Product section: repeated message and action hierarchy

- Evidence: [iPhone and following section](captures/apple-web-product-20260906.png).
- Observed: centered product identity and a short proposition precede two adjacent actions with different emphasis, followed by a large product rendering. A narrow separator starts the next product story. Space is concentrated around the image and proposition.
- Borrow for: introductions needing both explanation and purchase/signup routes. Keep action hierarchy consistent across sections; adapt it to the primary task instead of assuming purchase always needs the filled button.
- Tradeoff: sparse composition depends on useful assets and is wasteful when several specifications or records must be visible. The captured heading is partly behind sticky navigation after scrolling; do not reproduce that as a desired anchor behavior.

## Product grid: equal structure, different surfaces

- Evidence: [two-column product grid](captures/apple-web-grid-20260906.png).
- Observed: equal-width panels align titles, short benefit lines, paired actions, and large imagery. Light and dark panels coexist with the same hierarchy. Narrow gutters divide the panels; large rounded card containers are not used here.
- Borrow for: a small set of peer offerings with parallel facts and actions. Vary artwork or surface when it expresses a meaningful difference.
- Tradeoff: promotional panels do not replace a criterion-by-criterion comparison table. Pair the overview with explicit differences and recommendations when users need to choose.

## Comparison prompts

- Does the opening need one campaign proposition or several actionable entry points?
- Should users understand the offering first or proceed directly to an action?
- What facts must align across offers, and what details can wait?

Limits: desktop sections and scrolling only. Mobile adaptation, menus, checkout, performance, reduced motion, and keyboard accessibility were not tested. Captures are research evidence, not licensed product assets.
