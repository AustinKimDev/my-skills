# Social content calendar

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Read the user goal, account/channel restrictions, existing scheduled and published posts, production capacity and upcoming verified dates. Create a realistic calendar for the requested period rather than a quota. For each proposed slot show account, local time and timezone, audience need or source, topic, format, intended primary action, required assets and state: idea, draft, scheduled or published.

Check cross-platform brand frequency and conflicts in both directions, including existing future reservations. Do not assume any draft was scheduled. Mix formats only where it serves the audience and assets exist; avoid duplicate themes and forced daily output. Respect user-directed restrictions on each channel. Schedule only when specifically authorized and after confirming account and actual platform schedule state; log actual results and evidence in the authoritative record. Identify which ideas require further validation.
