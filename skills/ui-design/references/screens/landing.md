# Landing pages

Use product, campaign, pricing, event, and brand as optional lenses, illustrated in [catalog.json](catalog.json). Other purposes and mixed compositions are equally valid. Product pages explain value and starting; campaigns explain real eligibility/benefits; pricing supports cost comparison; events explain participation; brand pages communicate evidenced identity. Do not impose one section order on every page.

## Connections

Examples: product → service.auth/onboarding; pricing → service.checkout; event → service.form → service.result; campaign → service.detail. Define actual CTA destinations. Mark external or out-of-scope destinations; do not fill links with `#`.

## Discovery and comparison

Ask who arrives, from where, what they already know, why they hesitate, and which action matters. Research specific reference pages and recommend applicable patterns. During exploration, offer five directions by default, minimum four. Differentiate story, hierarchy, and interaction while preserving factual content. Large heroes, bento grids, scroll effects, and videos require a purpose.

Use layout/wireframe for evidence/action order, page for visual direction, and typography for real headlines and paragraphs. Follow up with relevant component, asset, or motion comparisons after the overall direction becomes concrete.

## Checks

Use better-writing for copy, better-layout for structure, and better-typography for reading. Generate/source images only when they serve content or brand. Never invent achievements, customer logos, or discount conditions. Verify the narrow first viewport, CTA destinations, content completeness, contrast, legibility, and purposeful motion.
