# Social profile audit

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Inspect the requested profile only in an authorized workspace and record the observed profile link, platform, date and relevant screen. Assess whether the name, bio, account identity, product status, links, pinned content and visual assets accurately explain who the account serves and what a visitor can do next. Inspect only fields actually visible; mark absent access as unknown, not a defect.

Prioritize concrete fixes by reader impact and effort. Give a before/after rewrite for the bio or pinned introduction where useful, preserving confirmed facts, contact terms and existing brand voice. Do not invent verified badges, operating status, customer metrics, media ownership or product functionality. Check current platform constraints in the interface when they matter, and do not use a numeric score as evidence of future growth.

A profile audit is read-only by default; make changes only within user authorization. Capture evidence without private data if possible and record actual changes separately from proposals.
