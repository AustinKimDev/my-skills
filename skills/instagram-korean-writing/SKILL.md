# Korean writing for Instagram

Make the visual and caption work together. Let the image carry a clear subject;
add context, useful information, or an appropriate next action in the caption
instead of transcribing every line on the image.

Read [voice notes](references/voice-notes.md) for observed examples,
counterexamples, and the official reference. The qualitative sample does not
prove that one caption style performs best.

## Start from the visual and purpose

Use the supplied image, carousel order, audience, established account voice, and
desired action. Inspect available images before making claims about them. With
no image available, work from the brief and distinguish proposed visuals from
inspected content; do not invent a photographed scene.

Select the requested deliverable: caption, carousel wording, story overlay, or
comment. A caption request does not require generating images or creating a
carousel.

For a new Korean public brand, friendly `~요` is a useful starting register.
Preserve formal wording for conditions, policies, credits, and official notices.
Keep an established personal voice. Do not force banmal, slang, emoji density,
or a stock influencer persona.

## Shape the text around the format

- **Feed caption:** connect the first line to the visible subject. Add the
  information or perspective the image cannot supply, then an action if needed.
  Remove generic admiration that could fit any image.
- **Carousel:** give the cover a specific subject or useful promise. Let each
  later card answer one question or carry one step. Preserve reading order;
  avoid splitting a sentence across cards or repeating the cover on every card.
  Keep essential conditions readable and add context in the caption.
- **Story overlay:** fit one idea to the intended visual. Do not describe a plain
  URL or an unconfigured destination as a working link sticker.
- **Comment:** answer the actual question in the account's register. Avoid
  pasting the full caption or inserting a sales CTA into every reply.

Separate context, practical information, and the next action with natural
paragraph breaks. Compact bullets help a checklist or event notice; a personal
reflection or photo caption does not need them by default.

## Match the CTA to the content

A checklist may invite an additional tip; a question may invite a specific
experience; a launch notice may point to a confirmed signup destination. Do not
require saves, follows, tags, shares, and comments together. Some posts need
no CTA.

Use `프로필 링크` only when the actual destination is confirmed. Check current
link behavior before claiming that a caption URL is clickable or a sticker can
be tapped. When unconfirmed, give an explicit destination without implying a
verified interaction. Verify current platform limits when final submission
requires them; do not hard-code an old hashtag or length rule.

## Preserve the factual and visual contract

- Keep supplied dates, places, application steps, eligibility, and credits exact.
  Shorten surrounding explanation without deleting conditions.
- Distinguish preparation, concept, demonstration, and released functionality.
  Do not create customer quotes, vote totals, attendance, or urgency.
- Keep generated imagery distinct from actual user work or testimonials.
  Original content guidance does not grant permission to reuse a photograph;
  retain the real permission and credit context.
- Use relevant hashtags when useful, rather than a generic pasted tag block.
  Make no unsupported reach promise.
- Write requested image descriptions from observed content. Alt descriptions
  and persuasive captions serve different purposes.

## Example transformation

Illustrative brief: four proposed checklist cards cover costume/wig/props,
logistics, and items to bring for a cosplay shoot. No live app feature is claimed.

```text
촬영 전날, 빠뜨린 준비는 없는지 같이 확인해요.

의상과 소품을 챙겼다면 일정과 장소, 사진을 전달받을 방법도 확인해 주세요.
준비할 때 꼭 챙기는 물건이 있나요? 댓글로 하나만 더해 주세요.
```

This original example adds context instead of repeating the whole checklist.
It is not a real customer's experience or an observed result.

## Deliver and review

Return the requested copy in a usable form. For a carousel, identify card order
and wording, then separate the caption. For a caption-only request, return the
caption without expanding the assignment.

Check the visual match, useful first line, natural register, preserved conditions,
and verified destination wording. This skill supplies no permission to publish,
schedule, alter profiles, contact others, or buy promotion.
