# Social hook

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Identify the audience, format, actual promise and source material. If analyzing an example, cite it and separate the observed opening from your interpretation. Identify its concrete subject, relevance, expectation and any missing context; never equate a successful post's opening with its cause.

Draft up to three genuinely different openings when alternatives are needed: an immediate problem, a concrete outcome, or an easy choice, each grounded in verified facts. Follow each with the substance it introduces so the payoff matches the promise. Choose the best fit for the audience and format rather than a universal formula. Do not fabricate results, urgency, controversy or personal experience.

Review for an identifiable reader, specific value and clarity at a glance. Preview the real platform display when available; character-based truncation is an estimate, not a verified screen. The [author of one hook tool](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill) reported weak winner prediction on a small YouTube Shorts sample. Do not present a heuristic score as a performance forecast. Output the opening, intended reader and rationale; label any source-based inference.
