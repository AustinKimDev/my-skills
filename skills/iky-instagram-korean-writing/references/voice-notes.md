# Observations behind the Korean Instagram defaults

Reviewed on 2026-10-02. Four Korean captions were read directly on public post
pages after locating them through search. This convenience sample focuses on
cosplay, photography, and a student club; it is not representative of Instagram
as a whole.

## Captions actually reviewed

| Source | Observed writing choice |
| --- | --- |
| [Ikigai club: preparation resource](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQqjfPmAbD7/) | Bracketed activity label, concise description of shared material, polite invitation and a single decorative emoji. |
| [Studio Banhari: cosplay portrait session](https://www.instagram.com/p/DLcePPPpsn7/) | Warm account-specific client/session commentary in polite language, enthusiastic punctuation and emojis, then a separate booking destination. |
| [san2_film: character shoot](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbK3p8wxecg/) | Work and character named first, personal colloquial observation about preparation, a relevant account tag and credit statement. The page presented video media. |
| [Hwaryugwan: cosplay session conditions](https://www.instagram.com/p/DLuX88KTorC/) | Named character and shoot first, then preparation, retouching, fee and costume conditions; booking instruction at the end. |

The table paraphrases register and structure without reproducing the captions.
It describes the text reviewed, not a complete visual audit of the media or
every card. Existing client experiences in these accounts are their claims;
they are not reusable testimonials for another brand.

## Official reference

[Instagram creators: original content guidelines](https://creators.instagram.com/original-content-guidelines)
was read directly. It distinguishes original creative contribution from simple
reposting or superficial alterations.

Use this as context for developing original material, not as a reach guarantee
or permission to reuse a photographer's work. Originality and permission are
separate questions. Check current official documentation when a deliverable
depends on current features or limits.

## Writing choices for a new draft

- Connect the opening to the specific work, character, activity, or useful
  information. Avoid a greeting that delays the subject.
- Let caption length follow the information. A resource invitation can be
  brief; a booking notice needs its actual conditions.
- Preserve the speaker's voice. The sample includes polite club wording, warm
  professional commentary, and personal colloquial language.
- When the brief supplies a visual, add context in the caption instead of
  duplicating all of its text. This is an editorial default, not an algorithm
  finding from the sample.
- Give the post an appropriate action, if any. A photographed result can stand
  alone; a booking notice needs a real booking destination.

Friendly `~요` is a proposed starting register for an unspecified public brand.
The sample does not establish it as Instagram's universal voice.

## Counterexamples and limits

The detailed studio notice contradicts a universal short-caption rule. The
personal photographer voice contradicts compulsory formal language. The club
invitation and studio notice show that the platform carries useful information
as well as aesthetic commentary. No fixed emoji, hashtag, or CTA count follows
from these observations.

Search ranking, personalized results and topic selection introduce bias. The
posts span different publication dates and purposes. Visible likes and comments
do not isolate the effects of wording or establish conversions. No performance
experiment or complete image/caption comparison was conducted.

The actual profile-link destination and caption-link behavior were not tested
by this review. Verify the user's account and current behavior before writing
interaction claims. Research supplies neither posting permission nor proof of
account setup.
