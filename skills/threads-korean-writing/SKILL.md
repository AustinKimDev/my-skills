# Korean writing for Threads

Write an actual thought, observation, question, or useful explanation. Preserve
the facts while choosing a conversational entry point when it fits the task.

For evidence, sampling limits, and alternative registers, read
[voice notes](references/voice-notes.md). Guidance about conversations does not
prove that a particular Korean ending or question produces growth.

## Identify the real speaker and thought

Use the brief, audience, existing account voice, and current state. Recover them
from the conversation before asking questions. The narrator may be an individual
creator, founder, team, or official account; do not substitute one for another.

A personal opening needs a factual basis. Do not invent a shoot, failed order,
customer conversation, late-night struggle, or founder anecdote to sound human.
A supplied plan or design decision can be discussed as a plan or question.

For a new public brand, friendly `~요` keeps the tone open without assuming
intimacy. Use banmal only when selected or established. Indexed Korean examples
include personal banmal and explanatory prose; neither is mandatory.

## Choose a useful conversation shape

- **A thought in progress:** state the issue, explain what makes it difficult
  or interesting, and optionally ask about one part of it.
- **An experience:** use only a real supplied experience. Share a detail and
  what the speaker learned, without fabricating a life lesson.
- **A practical explanation:** open with the problem or question, then answer
  with relevant nuance. Longer text is justified when the explanation needs it;
  compress repetition rather than enforcing a slogan-length limit.
- **A launch or operational notice:** preserve status, conditions, and the
  actual link. A factual notice can stay direct without a personal confession.
- **A reply:** answer the specific point and add useful context. Ask a follow-up
  only when it helps; omit a recurring promotional sign-off.

Stay on one thought. Use short natural paragraphs when the argument turns;
avoid breaking every clause onto its own line. A concrete question can make
answering easier, but is not a mandatory ending. Replace vague
`여러분은 어떻게 생각하세요?` with the actual choice when possible.

## Keep the voice relaxed without manufacturing familiarity

- Prefer specific everyday words to press-release framing and abstract claims.
- Use hesitations, `ㅎㅎ`, `ㅋㅋ`, or emojis only when they fit the actual speaker
  and mood. Do not sprinkle them into every sentence.
- Do not require `스친`, follow-for-follow language, forced praise, or universal
  banmal. Sampled creator voices are observations, not personas to copy.
- Avoid carrying over an Instagram hashtag bundle or inserting signup into
  every post. Verify current topic-label behavior if needed for submission.
- Invite a specific experience without requesting private details or pretending
  a poll, consensus, or audience relationship already exists.
- Do not invent controversy, urgency, vulnerability, or measured benefits to
  solicit replies.

For cross-platform adaptation, keep the factual contract and choose a new
conversational entry. Changing hashtags and line breaks alone is insufficient.
Preserve necessary conditions and the purpose of a commercial notice.

## Example transformation

Illustrative brief: a community is being designed around interests in works and
characters; the founder wants to understand how people meet cosplay friends.

```text
커뮤니티를 준비하면서 관심 작품과 캐릭터를 먼저 보여주려고 해요.
같은 작품을 좋아하는 사람을 알아보기 쉬웠으면 해서요.

코스프레 친구는 보통 어디서 만나세요?
```

This original illustrative draft describes preparation, not a live feature or
invented founder experience. If the speaker's real stance differs, change the
premise before polishing the rhythm.

## Deliver and review

Default to one ready-to-review post or reply. Add alternatives, follow-ups,
visual concepts, or analysis only when requested or necessary. Attach no
unsupported growth claim.

Check that the speaker is correct, the experience is real or clearly proposed,
the thought is concrete, the register fits the audience, and any question is
answerable. This skill supplies no permission to publish, schedule, message,
follow accounts, create a profile, or buy promotion.
