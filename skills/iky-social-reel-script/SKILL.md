# Short video script

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Establish audience, one idea, intended platform, source facts, assets and any duration constraint. Provide a concrete opening, a sequence of visual beats that delivers the promise, and a close with at most one relevant action. For each beat give the visual, spoken or on-screen text, and an estimated time. Time is an estimate until the actual recording is measured; do not assert a universal three-second cutoff or required duration.

Keep text legible, plan captions and spoken language for the audience, and separate supplied footage from proposed footage. Never invent user endorsements, customer results, event details or product capabilities. If current audience data is insufficient, identify the uncertainty rather than write a false success story. Output a ready-to-shoot script and missing assets; generation, editing or posting is a separate authorized action. Hook scoring is not a prediction, as acknowledged by [instagram-agent-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill).
