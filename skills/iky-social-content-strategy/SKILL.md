# Social content strategy

Read brand audience, verified service status, voice, recent posts, existing reservations, user constraints and audience research. Reply in Korean. Identify the reader's problem before choosing a format. Cite the observed question where available; mark an unsupported audience insight as a hypothesis and identify the next research step.

Develop distinct ideas that provide concrete value: a checklist, example, comparison, visual explanation, timely clarification or easy question. For each chosen idea specify audience, source, reader benefit, main point, format, platform, intended action, hypothesis and observation window. Match format to available truthful assets and the user's channel restrictions. When useful load the existing X, Threads or Instagram writing skill for native copy. Select one primary action; avoid repeated generic promotional questions.

Make the opening concrete without inventing facts, experiences, features or testimonials. Hooks are editing prompts, not success predictions: the [instagram-agent-skill author's small test](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill) did not reliably identify winners and used YouTube Shorts rather than Instagram. [Meta's creator guidance](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/) is platform guidance, not a universal ranking formula.

Choose the next slot after checking the user's cross-platform brand limits and existing calendar. Confirm the account immediately before any authorized posting. Record a draft, schedule or published post as its actual state with observed time and link. Preserve user content and existing permissions; measure the idea against a comparable baseline when one exists.
