# Niche research

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Define the relevant audience and a concrete research question. Select a few genuinely comparable public accounts or posts, and record account, direct URLs, date, format, topic and observable metrics. Note differences in audience size, publication age, distribution and whether content is sponsored. Respect platform rules and user's research-only restrictions; do not collect private interactions or post to external boards without permission.

Where several comparable posts from the same account at similar age are observable, a nonzero within-account median can give a descriptive outlier ratio; state sample count and missing data. Do not compare raw views across accounts as proof of an effective hook, or imply causal reasons. Avoid fake numerical examples and refrain from ranking if the baseline is too small.

Summarize the observed pattern, competing explanations and one original test for the user's audience with source links. Revisit periodically. [instagram-agent-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill) suggests within-account baselines, while its own validation documents limitations; use it as an idea rather than a proven causal method.
