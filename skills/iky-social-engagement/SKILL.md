# Relevant public conversations

Read brand voice, audience, user authorization, previous replies and account access. Reply in Korean. Inspect mentions, following feeds and search using the user-approved agent-owned workspace; never take over a user's workspace or bypass a handoff. Check the record and thread for duplicates.

Read the parent post and sufficient context to understand the actual question, speaker, recency and whether the brand can add something useful. Prefer a specific answer or clarification over generic promotion. Do not imply personal experiences the brand does not have, invent capabilities or endorsements, request private details, or add unrelated links. Public boards designated for research remain read-only without separate authorization.

When a suitable conversation exists and a public reply is authorized, confirm the active account immediately before sending. Keep participation proportionate to the user's limits. Verify the reply appears, and record parent and reply URLs, exact text, time, and a screenshot when feasible. If no conversation fits, do not force a reply: record searches and a useful next lead. Respect login restrictions and pending user decisions.

Follow up when it could help, checking actual responses and available metrics. Record observation time; distinguish zero from unavailable. A reply's exposure can depend on its parent account, so do not treat its views as a comparable original post or as conversion. [Meta's Threads guidance](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/find-your-community-with-new-threads-educational-insights/) reports general observations, not guaranteed results.
