# Audience and community research

Read the brand audience, verified product facts, prior findings, permissions and research question. Reply in Korean. Search relevant public discussions, mentions, following feeds and accessible social search using authorized tools and spaces. Respect community rules: research does not authorize posting. Avoid private messages and unnecessary personal information.

For each observation record the source link, date, audience, actual question, relevant context and a cautious interpretation. Separate an individual account from a repeated pattern; one post is not a trend. Check official sources before treating comments as factual rules about products, transport or events. Compare relevant peer content without copying it or assuming its results transfer.

Cluster needs and identify a useful next action: an answer to an actual question, a practical original post, or a research question. Prioritize relevance and ability to help over reach alone. Record source, suitability and next action in the existing project record. If nothing fits, say what was searched and leave a concrete next lead. Revisit sources periodically rather than the same board every hour. [Meta Threads guidance](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/find-your-community-with-new-threads-educational-insights/) contains platform-wide observations, not account-level guarantees.
