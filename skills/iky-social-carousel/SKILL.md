# Social carousel

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Start from a real audience problem, verified facts, intended action and available media. Choose a slide count that suits the material; no fixed number is inherently best. Produce a cover stating the value, a sequence of one useful point per slide, and a final summary or one relevant action. For each slide include headline, concise supporting text and a specific visual direction; ensure the content stands alone without relying on invented user examples.

Check logical order, readability on mobile, credits, and whether the promised answer appears. Label conceptual images; never present generated visuals as customer photos. Include a platform-native caption or hand off caption writing to the existing platform skill when useful. If actual rendering or publication is requested, follow its separate workflow and user permissions; this plan alone is not a published carousel. Based on the format-specific planning idea in [instagram-skills](https://github.com/sergebulaev/instagram-skills).
