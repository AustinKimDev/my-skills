# Korean writing for X

Make the subject and next action visible quickly. Fandom photo captions,
recruitment notices, announcements, and replies do not share one mandatory
tone or length.

For the observed examples and a requested tone analysis, read
[voice notes](references/voice-notes.md). This small qualitative sample is not
a rule about every Korean user or proof of reach.

## Choose the register from the brief

Use the established account voice and audience first. For a new public brand
with no selected register, start with friendly `~요`. Keep `~합니다` where an
announcement or service relationship calls for it. Use banmal only when the
user selects it or the account clearly establishes it; the platform alone does
not establish familiarity.

Recover the subject, availability, audience, and desired action from the supplied
brief or conversation. Ask only when a missing fact changes what readers would
believe or do. Never manufacture a launch date, participant count, scarcity,
endorsement, or customer reaction.

## Compose by post type

- **News or launch:** lead with the concrete subject, then its current status.
  Keep a prelaunch service in the preparation tense. Put the relevant link where
  the reader can find it without an unrelated preamble.
- **Recruitment or event:** separate confirmed audience, date, place, and
  application method into readable units. Preserve conditions even when they
  make the post longer. Keep unresolved logistics unresolved.
- **Photo or fandom post:** one specific observation can be enough when the
  image and audience supply the context. Use `최애` or `팀코` when that audience
  understands them. Recruitment or an offer still needs a clear subject.
- **Question:** ask about one concrete choice or experience. Make a reply
  possible without an essay or private information.
- **Reply or quote-post:** respond to the actual point and preserve whose
  experience it is. Avoid turning every reply into a promotion or representing
  quoted experience as the brand's own.

Start an ordinary update with a few short sentences. Expand for useful context
or necessary conditions. Break lines between ideas, not after every word.
Remove repetition before splitting a long brief into a thread; split only when
the substance needs it.

## Control the surface details

- Put the useful noun or question before generic greetings and hype.
- Let emojis signal mood or a section when useful. Their presence in sampled
  accounts does not establish a requirement or performance benefit.
- Use hashtags for a relevant work, event, or topic. Avoid a generic discovery
  bundle or fixed tag count. Event accounts may legitimately need several tags.
- Choose one primary action; do not routinely combine likes, reposts, follows,
  friend tags, comments, and site visits.
- Keep supplied image credits and context. If alt text is requested and the
  image is available, describe what it shows. Do not label a concept image as
  a real user's photograph.

For a submission-ready request, check the current account's actual post counter
or current official documentation. Plain character length does not prove that
Korean text, emojis, and URLs fit X's counting rules. Do not claim a verified
limit or fit without checking.

## Example transformation

Illustrative brief: a cosplay community is preparing for launch and wants one
interest question. No launch date or live app is available.

```text
다음에 해보고 싶은 캐릭터가 있나요?
작품이랑 캐릭터 이름을 하나씩 알려주세요.
```

This original example is not a quotation or an observed campaign result. For a
launch announcement, include preparation status and the confirmed destination;
do not attach that CTA to this question by habit.

## Deliver and review

Default to one ready-to-review draft. Add variants, a thread, alt text, or an
analysis report only when requested or necessary. Preserve required wording
and the factual contract when adapting another channel.

Check that the subject is immediate, required conditions survived compression,
the register fits the speaker, and the action matches the post. This skill writes
copy; it supplies no permission to publish, schedule, message, follow accounts,
alter profiles, or buy promotion.
