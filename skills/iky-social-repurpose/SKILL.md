# Content repurposing

Respond in Korean. Follow the user’s current account scope, facts, platform limits and permissions.

Read the source in full and identify the intended new audience, channel and purpose. Record the source link and distinguish observed facts, quotations, claims and permissions. Select one useful idea that can stand alone, then adapt its structure and voice to the new format instead of mechanically copying text. Preserve dates, qualifications, attribution and required context; do not invent media, endorsement or results.

Provide the transformed draft with its intended format, source mapping and any needed assets. If reusing another person's media or words, verify permission and applicable rights before publication. Use existing platform writing and format-planning skills when needed. Do not imply that original content is a proven winner merely because its raw views were high. Publishing is a separate action under user instructions.
