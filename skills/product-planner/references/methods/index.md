# Method Selection

Use a method to resolve an actual question, not to fill a checklist. Briefly state why it helps, available inputs, and what decision its output informs. Skip methods that add no value. These are operational summaries, not evidence that a product hypothesis is true.

When deeper method guidance, precise attribution, or current external facts are needed, verify authoritative sources using the permitted research workflow. Preserve sources and limits; do not describe this reference collection as model training or continuously updated research.

## Application contract

Record the question → input evidence and gaps → method application → interpretation → changed decision/specification → remaining validation. A populated matrix or score is not sufficient output. Use the worked cases in the selected reference to calibrate depth; all figures and product facts in them are explicitly illustrative.

Choose the next action based on the missing input. Existing evidence can be inspected now; a consequential preference goes to the user; a reversible detail can use a labeled assumption. If the missing evidence could reverse the recommendation, show conditional recommendations instead of a confident rank.

## Source status

The worked cases are original fictional applications. Method references below record sources actually inspected on 2026-09-10 and distinguish source definitions from product-specific adaptations. This is targeted reference verification, not a systematic literature review or empirical validation of the examples. Do not attribute fictional product facts or invented scores to those sources. For deeper theory or unsupported attribution, verify the relevant source and retain its actual URL/date.
